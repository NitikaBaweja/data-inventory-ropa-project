\f0\fs24 \cf0 # Data Inventory and ROPA Privacy Governance Project\
\
## Project Overview\
\
This project demonstrates practical privacy governance and data protection capabilities through the creation of a comprehensive Data Inventory and Record of Processing Activities (ROPA) aligned with Article 30 of the :contentReference[oaicite:0]\{index=0\}.\
\
The project simulates a mid-sized e-commerce organization and documents how personal data is collected, processed, stored, shared, retained, and protected across business functions including Sales, Marketing, Customer Support, Human Resources, Finance, and IT.\
\
In addition to the core privacy documentation, the project includes:\
- A Data Flow Diagram\
- An Executive Summary\
- A Privacy Governance Dashboard built in :contentReference[oaicite:1]\{index=1\}\
\
---\
\
## Project Objectives\
\
- Create a complete Data Inventory of systems containing personal data\
- Develop a GDPR Article 30 Record of Processing Activities (ROPA)\
- Map data flows between internal systems and third-party processors\
- Identify high-risk processing activities\
- Analyze retention periods and international transfers\
- Build an executive privacy dashboard\
- Present findings in an executive summary\

## Methodology\
\
### 1. Data Inventory\
Identified all applications, databases, and third-party tools that process personal data, including CRM, payment processors, marketing platforms, HR systems, and customer support tools.\
\
### 2. Record of Processing Activities (ROPA)\
Documented each processing activity with:\
- Purpose of processing\
- Data subjects\
- Personal data categories\
- Legal basis\
- Systems used\
- Third parties\
- International transfers\
- Retention periods\
- Risk levels\
- DPIA requirements\
\
### 3. Data Flow Mapping\
Created a visual diagram showing how personal data moves from customers and employees through internal systems and external processors.\
\
### 4. Privacy Risk Assessment\
Assessed processing activities based on sensitivity, data volume, international transfers, and regulatory exposure.\
\
### 5. Executive Dashboard\
Built an interactive dashboard in Google Looker Studio to visualize privacy KPIs and risk indicators.\

## Key Findings\
\
1. Most customer-facing processing activities rely on Contract or Consent as the legal basis.\
2. Payment processing, fraud detection, and security monitoring represent the highest-risk areas.\
3. Several activities involve international transfers to the United States and other countries.\
4. Special category data is processed in employee benefits and whistleblower reporting.\
5. Retention periods range from 90 days to 10 years.\
\
